# 🌱 EcoGuard AI
### AI-Powered Smart Waste & Methane Risk Intelligence Platform

**Tagline:** Predict Waste. Detect Risk. Simulate Action. Protect the Future.

EcoGuard AI is a climate-tech solution designed to improve waste management by predicting waste generation, identifying potential organic waste accumulation hotspots, and supporting smarter waste collection decisions.

The platform connects smart waste management with climate awareness by explaining how accumulated organic waste can generate methane under oxygen-limited conditions, contributing to global warming.

## 🌍 Problem Statement

Traditional waste management often relies on fixed collection schedules and manual reporting. This can lead to:

- Overflowing waste bins and delayed collection.
- Poor waste segregation.
- Accumulation of organic waste.
- Unhygienic surroundings and environmental pollution.
- Increased methane emission risks from decomposing organic waste.

## 💡 Our Solution

EcoGuard AI provides a smart waste management platform that uses available waste data, location information, predictive analytics, and risk indicators to help communities make better waste collection decisions.

The platform aims to support timely collection, responsible waste segregation, and climate-conscious waste management.

## ✨ Key Features

- **📊 Smart Dashboard:** View waste management indicators and area-level statistics.
- **🔮 Waste Generation Prediction:** Estimate future waste quantities using available data and prediction methods.
- **🗺️ Waste Hotspot Identification:** Highlight locations with potential waste accumulation risks.
- **♻️ Waste Segregation Guidance:** Encourage separation of organic and non-organic waste.
- **🌡️ Methane Risk Awareness:** Explain environmental risks associated with accumulated organic waste.
- **🚛 Collection Planning:** Support better collection scheduling based on predicted waste and reported conditions.
- **📍 Citizen Reporting:** Allow users to report waste-related problems.
- **🔄 Waste Journey Tracking:** Represent the journey of waste from collection to its intended destination.
- **🧪 What-If Simulator:** Explore how changes in collection frequency or waste handling may affect risk indicators.
- **🌐 Multilingual Interface:** Support English, Tamil, and Hindi where translations are implemented.

## 🌱 Climate-Tech Impact

Organic waste can produce methane when it decomposes in oxygen-limited environments, such as certain landfill conditions.

Methane is a potent greenhouse gas that contributes to climate change.

EcoGuard AI promotes:
- Earlier identification of waste accumulation.
- Better organic waste segregation.
- Improved waste collection planning.
- Greater awareness of methane and climate change.
- More responsible waste management practices.

**Note:** Methane risk indicators are estimates, not direct methane measurements, unless supported by appropriate sensors and validated data.
